# Feedback closure: RealNews 1.0.7 and Shazamer 1.0.4

The seven distinct relevant records in feedback.json, list.json and lists.json are #44, #47, #49, #54, #55, #59 and #62. Their original historical status fields have not been changed.

| Reports | Resolution and evidence |
| --- | --- |
| #44: local metadata times | One formatter is shared by the article and All Fields. Each raw field is parsed separately, so published and updated retain different instants. Invalid values and original model/XML values are preserved. A fixture with 14:00 +0300 and 16:00 Z displays 13:00 and 18:00 on both test devices. |
| #44: white favicon contrast | Every feed has a Gray icon background switch in Add/Edit. Legacy feeds default to on, and on/off round trips through the saved feed model. On the iPad, the actual switch and Save button were tapped, the app restarted, off remained saved, and the original on setting was restored. |
| #49: image Close | Image tap recognizers explicitly reject toolbar/control touches and no longer delay touch beginnings. Actual UIKit began/ended events were delivered to the rendered Close button on the iOS 6 iPad; the image modal closed and the feed list returned, without a crash. This is not a direct call to closeTapped or dismissal from the presenting controller. |
| #55: unavailable resources | The existing 1/2/3/5/10 second setting now also bounds the whole request across redirects, DNS/TLS and slow body delivery. The timeout signals cancellation without freeing TLS state on another thread. Both devices were tested against local controlled servers: no HTTP response (1 and 10 s), a byte every 0.4 s (3 s), repeated delayed redirects (2 s), and a stalled TLS handshake (2 s). All timed out near the selected deadline while the main-thread timer continued at about 20 ticks/s. A healthy response succeeded afterwards. |
| #44/#62: icon corners | All 13 icon sizes in both architectures of both apps have transparent corners. The old RealNews repository logo was replaced with the exact rounded icon from the released app; public/source/local repo copies match. |
| #47/#54: repository/RSS availability | The repository feed, its image, both app pages, both public app icons and both architecture packages are available. RealNews on the iPad actually displays the repository's blue S favicon. |
| #59/#62: Shazamer recording crash | The fingerprint stack fix and native sanitizer/small-stack check were released earlier. Real recognition of NEXTIME's СВЕТЛАНА! passed on iOS 6 and iOS 16. Shazamer 1.0.4 also retained the same process after handoffs to all three receivers on the signed-in iPhone. No Shazamer code change was necessary for this closure. |

Both RealNews architectures and the Foundation feedback regression tests passed CI. The final feed-editor layout was additionally checked on both devices; the Find button stays inside its field, and the new switch and labels fit the screen.

Hardware: iPad 2, iOS 6.1.3; rootless iPhone X, iOS 16.7.16. Exact reporter models and every supported OS version were not available. These checks close the reported reproducible cases on the available hardware; they are not a claim of exhaustive bug-free behavior.

The device regression bridge requires a local Documents/feedback-checks flag, which is removed after testing. Its pre-iOS-8 touch-event support is adapted from [KIF](https://github.com/kif-framework/KIF), with Apache-2.0 notices bundled in Resources and docs/third-party. It does not publish a network service or add test fixtures to the user's feeds.
